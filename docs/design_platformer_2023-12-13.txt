Genre

         Heaven's Fall is a 2d platformer.
In most 2d platformers, you fall down, and walk on the floor, but what if you could walk on walls
and ceilings. That paired with the ability to jumping momentum into running momentum and vice
versa allows for a fast paced smooth platforming experience. As the player plays through the
game and learns how to fully use the movement system, they will be able to complete the
platforming challenges and make it further into the game.

Narrative

         Rayne is a salvager who climbs crashed colony ships salvaging parts in order to build a
working space ship that is able to leave the solar system. He finds a ship that made a crash
landing in the desert and must climb it to find a computer in the control module. When inside he
hears a mysterious voice that tempts him with promises of moving between stars in mere
minutes. Rayne finds the place has been booby trapped by the natives as he finds spikes
everywhere. As Rayne gets closer and closer to the control room, he starts to see strange
paintings on the wall. When he gets to the control room he finds a strange pedestal with a crystal
on it. The voice tells him to go and touch the crystal if he wants to become a god. The player then
will move towards the crystal and when they touch it, the game fades to black with a final line
from both of the characters in unison "let the heavens fall."

  1. Challenge- It is impossible to reach the exit by just walking and jumping to it, the player
      will have to use mag-switch in order to reach the exit of each room while avoiding spikes
      along the way.

  2. Learn- By experimenting with mag-switch and the movement system, the player will learn
      how to build up speed and clear jumps as well as avoid spikes.

  3. Reward- Part of the reward for learning the movement system is the enjoyment from
      moving fast and the satisfaction of moving fast through the level. I also hope to make the
      story a reward for reaching the new levels so the mystery can draw players in.

Mechanics

     Movement
              Running- done by pressing A, D, W, or S. Depending on which wall the player is on
                  they will be able to run either up and down or left and right.
              Aerial drift- done by pressing A or D while in the air, velocity cap will be reached
                  in half a second. After the x velocity cap is exceeded, acceleration in that
                  direction will no longer function, but acceleration in the opposite direction is
                  enabled.
              Friction- your horizontal speed will be decreased when the player stops running.
              Jumping- done by pressing SPACEBAR. While the spacebar is held down the
                  gravitational acceleration is lower than without it held down. This allows for
                  greater control of vertical movement.

   Mag-switch

  This mechanic is the crux of the game and I believe this video is the most concise way to

show it off. https://youtu.be/SP-c2Cniguk

  Mag-switch allowes the player to walk on the red walls and platforms. Depending on which

surface the player is walking on, either left and right or up and down walking will be disabled. The

player will automatically be pulled into mag-switch when they touch a steel wall and

automatically exit it when they jump or otherwise loose contact with the surface. Mag-switch will

allow the player to jump from a higher position or carry momentum from jumping into running up

a wall in order to move fast. Every stage will be designed around this mechanic and the player

must interact with it in order to beat the game.

 Game state end and reset-
          The game state will end once the end of the last room is reached, or the ending
             cutscene plays, or back to main menu is selected from the pause menu.
          Lives, death and respawn- The player will die in one hit. The player will have
             unlimited lives, though a limit may be needed for a smooth experience at the
             showcases, the player will automatically respawn at the beginning of the room
             they are currently in.

 Stage elements
          Mag-field. They are invisible hitboxes that change your mag-switch variable. The
             color of the surface you are trying to walk on will indicate if there is a mag field
             there.
          Steel blocks. They are the standard floor and walls. They are able to be walked on
             and have a mag field next to them.
          Aluminum Blocks. They are nonmagnetic walkable surfaces that do not have a mag
             field. They are used to create one way pathways and other obstacles in the game.
          Spikes. They are a one hit kill entity that acts as a punishment for failing a
             platforming challenge. They are made of aluminum but will look much newer than
             the other aluminum assets have very little corrosion to tell them apart.
          Checkpoint. They will set your spawn point. These may be in the 2nd build of the
             game.

 Camera and visual effects

                                 Level design

  The game is split up into different rooms. Each room will only have one exit and the goal is

to reach that through platforming. Each room will have walls, platforms, and level elements. They

will be color coded blue for aluminum which mag-switch doesn't work on, and red for steel which

the mag-switch works on. The spikes will be instant kill and serve as a way to avoid players just

walking on the ceiling to solve all the puzzles. Some platforms will have gaps that are not

possible to make from the ground or a standing jump and need momentum to be built up before

hand in order to cross the gap.

  There will be 5 rooms total, The first 2 rooms that will be ready for the first showcase will

be based on sketches I have made in pre production. These levels will take place in the

construction yard. That way the team will have a decisive vision to start out with and will have

time to learn the game's mechanics before designing the next ones. Right after the first

showcase, everyone will submit their puzzle ideas and we will vote on which ones to implement in

the next batch of 2 levels. The same will apply with the 5th level which will be ready for the last

milestone. The final level will be the culmination of all of the feedback and experience we have.

Cutscenes

  During cutscenes, the game will freeze and the music will slow down and be pitch shifted.

The closed captions will appear at the bottom of the stage, with black text indicating Rayne and

red text indicating the mysterious voice. The intro cutscene will be different in that it will be a

text crawl similar to the star wars intro being narrated by Rayne. Each cutscene should never

exceed 30 seconds and be able to be skipped by pressing esc.

  Speedrun timer

  During gameplay a timer will start when start is selected and last until the end of the

game. I don't want to kick people out of the game, but for the demos, we may need to implement

a 10 min time limit so that people can have a smooth playing experience.

Schedule

    Heaven's fall schedule

Goals for each milestone.

    1. Have the game in playable state with core mechanics working and 2 rooms built. Spikes
         excluded. Have finished sprites but relatively lackluster animations.

    2. Have all main features including death, respawn, checkpoints in the game and 4 rooms
         implemented. Have working animations for everything but mag-switch, have working intro
         cutscene.

    3. Have all features implemented and all animations done. Have cutscenes 1-3 done. Have
         the full mvp done and make the game have a functional ending.
