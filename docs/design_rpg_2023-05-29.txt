Let The Heavens Fall RPG

Unity

(Quick Note: Green highlighted text is core features, yellow highlighted text is polish).

Genre

Let the Heavens Fall is an RPG with fighting game elements sprinkled in. It has the overworld
exploration and presentation of a late 90s j-rpg and the combat mechanics of a fighting game.

Mechanics
         The main selling point of the game is the combo mechanic. This allows players to create

their own special attacks and gives them the freedom that classic RPG games lack. The combo
duration is governed by the combo gauge. This depletes at a constant rate whenever the opponent
is not in hit stun and by a small amount that varies between attacks. There will be no rng involved
in the combat system whatsoever, the player will live and die by their own skill and preparation.
For players with less skill who are less skilled or able, we will make auto combos that are
unlocked through leveling up. Much like Paper Mario or the Mario and Luigi series, all enemy
attacks can either be dodged or blocked to reduce or completely mitigate all damage. A block
button is vital to this, and a Perry mechanic may be implemented as a stretch goal. The overworld
will contain basic cardinal movement, with more advanced options such as jumping, using
weapons to interact with the environment, and diagonals possibly being added as stretch goals. I
want to make this game take up the least of the player's time possible so the party will be healed
fully after each encounter and fleeing a fight will work every time and reset your consumable
items to before you initiated the fight.

                                                  Gameplay Loop
         The main gameplay loop is driven by the story and world. The player will have to explore
their area and defeat the required enemies in combat in order to progress. Along the way, they
will level up to get new automatic combos, find new equipment and weapons (along with different
weapon types that change up the play-style of characters), and unlock new characters. Each
mandatory story boss will give a health upgrade and will be the only way to increase your hp pool.

This is because I have found that doing optional content in RPGs often results in a less

challenging and memorable boss fight than rushing straight toward a boss. This method makes

balance much easier since each player will approach the late-game bosses with the exact same

amount of health. Mandatory equipment and upgraded main weapons will be dropped after every

major boss as well so that minimalists will not be punished but more powerful items will be

sprinkled around the world to reward exploration.

1. The player receives information from the main story to go somewhere or do something.
2. The player will have to explore the overworld in order to figure out how to accomplish

    their task through environmental storytelling.
3. The player moves around the overworld exploring until they find something of interest

    (e.g. a cave, house, hill they can climb or enemy to fight)
4. The player initiates a fight with the opponent and enters battle mode.
5. Depending on the type of encounter, the player gets to act first or is ambushed and has to

    face an attack. Acting gives the player the choice to attack, combo, item, or flee. Item lets
    the player use a one-time consumable which may heal them, give them a buff, or special
    attack when pressed in attack or combo.
6. Once the player has finished their turn they will have to defend from enemy attacks some
    blocked and some dodged.

Art design
The graphics will use a 3d environment with 2d pixel art for the characters in a similar vein to
Xenogears, Octopath traveler, generation 4 and 5 Pokemon, and Star Ocean. The aesthetic I am
going for in this game is to blend medieval fantasy with hard sci-fi using concepts such as O'neal
cylinders, and mech suits. I want the technology in the game to feel like an actual engineer
designed it rather than a piece of art while still maintaining some artistic elements such as color
theory to match the characters and factions that use them.

User interface
The goal of the UI in the overworld is to be as unintrusive as possible while giving the proper
information to the player for any given situation. The minimap will be visible in the top right
corner at all times with triangles at the edge for objectives if the player chooses to enable them.
Since the player's health will be refilled between every battle and experience only affects auto
combos, this will be the only thing that shows up on the overworld. It will also show enemies so
the player knows when an encounter will happen and to avoid it if they wish. Other than that the
overworld ui will simply show the gameplay. The battle UI will show the health of your characters
and the health of the boss as well as the combo gauge and the finisher gauge as well. The
character health bars will be circular like a clock on the top left of the screen but the boss will
have a linear health bar in the top middle.

The game takes place 11,000 years in the future in an alien world and the orbiting space habitats

after a cataclysm that killed all sentient life in the Galaxy. The inhabitants of this world are

descendants of humans grown from genetic material from the Genesis system created by the

artificial intelligence Leviticus. The demo takes place in a space habitat orbiting the planet one of

the Exodus fleet spaceships constructed before the fall of humanity. The main character Paul is a

teenager who runs away from his parents and falls into a hole in the habitat he lives in. Inside, he

finds the lush soil has given away to metal and wires. He finds a device that holds a backup of the

man who defeated the aliens oppressing humanity and who caused the cataclysm that ended the

cycle of gods. The machines in the cavern suddenly come to life and attach a device to Paul's

brain stem. When he comes to, he meets John, a military veteran and former knight of the

"second law" faction who is searching for the man contained in the device. The ai Leviticus is

amassing an army to enact revenge on the knight of the second law and build itself a new body.

John and Paul try to get back to their spaceship, but John is notified that it has been destroyed.

Seeing no other option, they make their way to the king's palace and plan to bargain with him to

use an emergency spacecraft to get to the surface. They arrive at the palace and are greeted with

the place in disorder and completely vacant. The goal is to figure out what happened in the palace

and defeat the Levites (Leviticus army). The leader of the Levites is Cain, the brother of John who

was left behind during a skirmish between the Second Law and the kingdom of Jeherico. Seeking

revenge they engage in a heated battle. After being disarmed Cain summons his mech, and a

second battle ensues.

Goal

The goal of the game is for the player to kill the members of the enemy faction and save the king.
To drain their opponent's hp before they drain yours. The demo will have 3 fights in it.

The goal of this game is for the player to use their character's unique tools in combination with
their assist character to defeat their opponent. The player can explore the nuances of each
character to develop new strategies and techniques to gain advantages over their opponents. The

Controls

Let the Heavens Fall will be played with a keyboard or controller. Let the Heavens Fall uses a grid
system of movement similar to Pokemon to traverse the overworld. The player is able to move in
the four cardinal directions during overworld gameplay. The main selling point of this game lies in
its combat. The game functions like a traditional turn-based RPG except for one feature, the
COMBO mechanic. This turns the game into a 2D fighting game for a turn and allows the player to
start a combo. The shift key will allow the player to activate a character's special ability. For Paul,
this is to slow down time (we just cut velocity, acceleration, and maximum speed temporarily and
multiply it when the special ability is over). And for John, it is an air dash that allows him to
extend combos.

Mechanics

While the controls have already been explained, it is important for me to properly dissect the
mechanics of the game. Fighting games are a legacy genre with several rich mechanics, so it is
important to highlight the specifics of how each mechanic will work. (Please remember that when
I say left or right, it is under the presumption that the player character is facing towards the
right.)

Movement

Skip, (John only): done by pressing shift, works in 8 directions
Chris Hancock                   10

5/29/2023                 

Jumping: Performed by hitting up. The player will jump into the air. There will be a very short
window before the jump where the player is vulnerable. This is called pre-jump. Every character
can also perform a double jump by hitting up again while in the air. A double jump cannot be
performed if the player has already executed an airdash.

Attacking and Offense

Assist: Performed by hitting the R1 button. The assist will switch your player-controlled character
and attack in order to extend the combo. The assist will cycle clockwise from the party setup
depending on who you picked for the start of the fight. The player will be forced to switch to the
next character no matter what at the end of the round.
Combo finishers: Strong attack

Highs, Mids, and Lows: The three types of attacks each normal and special can be. Whether or not
a move is a low, mid, or high is very important because it determines how the opponent can block
each move. Lows can be blocked by crouching blocking only. If the opponent is standing up, they
are susceptible to lows. Mids can be blocked while crouching or standing. If the opponent is
holding  or  (left or back left, respectively). High can be blocked by stand blocking only. If the
player is crouching, they are susceptible to highs.

Defense

Blocking: Defends the player from damage. Performed by holding either left or down left ( or 
respectively). Down left allows the player to crouch block. This will defend them from lows and
mids. Left allows the player to stand block. This defends them from mids and highs. If the player
is not stand blocking while holding back, they will simply walk backwards.

Meters and Gauges

Health: The amount of health that each player has. Health is depleted by attacking the opponent
with attacks. Once damage is dealt, it cannot be undone in that battle. Any health lost is lost
permanently until the next fight.

Combo finisher gauge: this builds up as you land hits on the opponent. There is a tradeoff because
this guage is based off of hits so the weaker multi hit combos are better at building this gauge
than the stronger combos with fewer hits.
